# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Hiremory to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone — regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or how they found the project.

## Our standards

**Encouraged:** being respectful, giving constructive feedback, assuming good intent, and
helping newcomers get unstuck.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualized language,
trolling, publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing
the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, or warn or ban contributors who violate this code.
